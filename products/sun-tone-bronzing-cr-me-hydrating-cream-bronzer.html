Westman Atelier

Sun Tone Bronzing Crème Hydrating Cream Bronzer

$75.00
Acne-Safe Verified
Why I picked it

Westman Atelier's Sun Tone Bronzing Creme gives you two shades for the perfect natural sun-kissed tan. The lighter shade goes on the high points where sunlight would naturally hit, and the deeper one defines cheekbones, temples, and jawline. The cream-to-powder formula glides on, blends easily, and settles to a soft hydrated-matte finish. Founded by editorial makeup artist Gucci Westman, it's the kind of bronzer that shows decades of artistry in a small compact.

Best for

All skin types.

How I use it

Work in to a small fluffy brush and then apply to the face. The lighter shade is perfect for adding color on the high points of the face like the forehead/temples, cheeks, and nose, and then use the deeper shade to add more definition in the hollows of the the cheeks and jawline.

One honest note

Westman Atelier is a luxury makeup brand and definitely a splurge. It's worth every penny but if you don't wear makeup or bronzer often, other more affordable options like the Cheek Kiss Cream Bronzer from Milani will do the trick.
