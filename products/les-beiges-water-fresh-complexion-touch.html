Chanel

LES BEIGES Water-Fresh Complexion Touch

$72.00
Acne-Safe Verified
Why I picked it

The fragrance-free and alcohol-free formula is sixty percent water, built as a lightweight gel, with jasmine extract for antioxidant protection and tamarind seed extract for hydration through the day. Coverage is medium and it works two ways: pressed all over for an even complexion, or placed only where you need it.

Best for

Dry to normal skin that wants a fresh, undone finish and the option to build coverage only where it is needed.

How I use it

1-1.5 pumps spread on forehead and cheeks to release the micro-droplet pigments, then blended in with a damp sponge for light coverage or a foundation brush for medium coverage.

One honest note

The price is steep for it's size, and especially for a product that's 60% water. With that said, the lightweight, skincare-like formula wears beautifully and provides an impressive amount of coverage.

The brush is included.
